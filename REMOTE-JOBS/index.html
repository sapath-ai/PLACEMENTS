01 — 2026/27 AI Role Architecture
Updated August 2026

The AI Engineering Roles That Actually Matter Now

The market has moved beyond a single generic “AI Engineer” title. Modern AI roles increasingly converge around agent systems, AI platforms, inference, evaluation, security, multimodal systems, and production software engineering. This taxonomy is organized by the capability you own—not by hype or a job-title keyword.

2026 Market Signal: Current roles are increasingly asking engineers to connect AI models to production systems: agent orchestration, MCP/tool integration, retrieval, evaluation, observability, security, Kubernetes/cloud infrastructure, inference and cost control. Recent 2026 postings from Arm, Accenture, Teradyne, SingleStore, AWS and others reflect this convergence. This roadmap therefore treats AI Systems Engineering as the core career family and specialization as the differentiator.
Important taxonomy change: “Prompt Engineer” is no longer treated as a primary destination. Prompting is a capability inside AI application, agent, evaluation and product engineering. Likewise, “MLOps” is increasingly merging into the broader AI Platform Engineering discipline. Salary ranges below are directional US-market reference bands only—not guarantees—and remote eligibility varies by country, employment model and company.
