Elite Track · Competitive Programming

CP Extreme — the HFT problem-solving engine

Competitive Programming is not treated here as a résumé decoration or a LeetCode checklist. It is a deliberate-practice system for building speed, abstraction, algorithmic judgment, implementation accuracy, debugging discipline, and performance under pressure.

Standard:Do not confuse “I have seen the algorithm” with mastery. A topic is not cleared until you can derive the idea, implement it from a blank file, prove correctness, analyze complexity, survive adversarial tests, and reproduce the technique under a contest clock.
The CP mastery contract
Understand
Derive the core idea and identify when it applies. No pattern-name memorization without mechanism.
Implement
Write the technique from an empty editor. Templates may be earned only after the concept is understood.
Prove
State the invariant, correctness argument, and asymptotic complexity before calling the problem solved.
Stress-test
Construct edge cases, adversarial cases, maximum constraints, duplicate-heavy inputs, and pathological structures.
Time-box
Repeat the skill under progressively tighter clocks until recognition and implementation become automatic.
Generalize
Solve variants where the obvious template fails. Explain what changed and why the old method breaks.

You may record hypotheses and dead ends, but cannot consult external solutions. The objective is independent reasoning.

A second run must reproduce the reported benchmark and research result within a defined tolerance.

You must allocate time rationally. Burning 90 minutes on one elegant dead end is treated as a strategic failure.
