The AI world is flooding medicine with unprecedented capability — but most engineers understand the technology without understanding the domain. That gap is your opportunity. This roadmap builds you into someone who understands both the biology and the algorithms, both the clinical workflow and the model architecture. You will specialise in two of the most consequential frontiers: general healthcare AI (medical imaging, clinical NLP, drug discovery, genomics) and the AI of the human brain and nervous system — computational neuroscience, BCI, neural decoding, and neurological disease modelling. The combination is rare. Rare is irreplaceable.

1.1 Human Biology & Physiology for AI Engineers
You need enough biology to read a medical paper and know what matters. This is not about becoming a doctor — it's about not being ignorant in the room.
Anatomy & Organ Systems
Cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, hepatic. Understand what each organ measures, regulates, and signals. Know the biomarkers (troponin, creatinine, HbA1c) that appear in clinical datasets.
BiologyClinical
Cellular & Molecular Biology
DNA → RNA → Protein. Gene expression, transcription factors, epigenetics. Essential for genomics AI and drug discovery — you cannot build what you cannot explain.
GenomicsFoundations
Neuroscience Fundamentals
Neuron morphology, action potentials, synaptic transmission, neurotransmitters. The structure of the cortex, basal ganglia, cerebellum. This is your neuro primer — you will go very deep later.
NeuroPriority
Pathophysiology
How diseases disrupt normal function. Cancer biology, neurodegeneration (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's), diabetes, cardiovascular disease. AI solves problems — you need to know what the problems actually are.
DiseaseClinical
Resources

1.2 Medical Data Types & Healthcare Systems
Healthcare data is messy, structured differently from everything else, and governed by regulations engineers usually ignore until it bites them.
Medical Imaging Modalities
X-ray (projection), CT (Hounsfield units, slices), MRI (T1/T2/FLAIR/fMRI/DWI), PET, Ultrasound. Understand the physics and what each modality reveals vs hides. This directly governs which architectures work.
ImagingTechnical
Clinical Data Standards
HL7, FHIR (SMART on FHIR), DICOM (for images), ICD-10/11 (diagnoses), SNOMED CT, LOINC (lab codes), CPT. These are the lingua franca of hospital data. Ignorance here costs months.
StandardsInteroperability
EHR & Clinical Workflow
Electronic Health Records (Epic, Cerner), clinical notes, SOAP format, medication orders, lab orders. Understanding clinical workflow tells you where AI fits — and where it will be ignored.
ClinicalWorkflow
Biomedical Signal Data
ECG (12-lead), EEG (10–20 system), EMG, PPG, SpO2. Temporal, multichannel, noisy. These signals are the raw material of computational neuroscience and wearable health AI.
SignalsNeuro
Resources & Tools
PhysioNet (free ECG/EEG datasets) MIMIC-IV (clinical ICU data) DICOM standard — NEMA docs FHIR R5 (with R4/R4B interoperability) — HL7.org pydicom, hl7apy (Python libs)
1.3 Regulatory & Ethical Landscape
AI that cannot be deployed is worthless. The best engineers know what it takes to get a system approved and trusted in a clinical setting.
FDA & CE Mark (AI/ML)
FDA's AI/ML-based Software as Medical Device (SaMD) framework, predetermined change control plans, 510(k) vs PMA. Europe's EU AI Act + MDR. These determine what you can ship.
RegulatoryFDA
HIPAA, GDPR & Data Ethics
De-identification, data use agreements, consent frameworks, federated learning as a privacy solution. You will be trusted with the most sensitive human data that exists — treat it accordingly.
PrivacyEthics
Fairness & Bias in Healthcare AI
Pulse oximetry bias in dark skin. Dermatology datasets skewed toward fair skin. Chest X-ray models trained on US data failing globally. Know where bias hides and how to measure and mitigate it.
FairnessCritical

2.1 Medical Imaging AI
The most commercially mature domain. Radiology, pathology, ophthalmology, dermatology — all transformed by vision AI. This is where most healthcare AI jobs live today.
Radiology AI
CNNs and Vision Transformers for chest X-ray (pneumonia, TB, pneumothorax), CT (lung nodule detection, intracranial haemorrhage), MRI brain tumour segmentation (BraTS dataset). The gold standard pipelines.
CNNViTSegmentation
Computational Pathology
Whole Slide Imaging (WSI), Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) for tumour grading, H&E stain analysis. Models like UNI and CONCH — foundation models trained on millions of pathology slides. This is an exploding field.
PathologyMILFoundation Models
Ophthalmology AI
Diabetic retinopathy grading (the first FDA-approved autonomous AI diagnostic — IDx-DR), glaucoma detection from fundus photos, AMD staging. One of the highest-precision domains in the field.
RetinaFDA-approved
3D Medical Image Segmentation
nnU-Net (the self-configuring segmentation framework that beats almost everything), MONAI (PyTorch-based medical imaging library), SAM-Med (Segment Anything adapted for medicine). Organ and tumour delineation for treatment planning.
nnU-NetMONAISAM
Multimodal Imaging Fusion
Combining CT + PET, MRI + genomics, imaging + clinical text. The future of radiology AI is not single-modality — it's holistic, cross-modal understanding. Learn cross-attention and late fusion strategies.

The explosion of medical LLMs is the biggest shift in healthcare AI right now. Clinical notes contain more diagnostic information than any other data source.
Medical NLP Fundamentals
Named Entity Recognition for clinical concepts (diseases, drugs, dosages), relation extraction, negation detection (is this symptom present or absent?), temporal reasoning in notes. spaCy + scispaCy, cTAKES.

Deep learning for DNA sequence modelling (Enformer, Evo 2, AlphaGenome), variant effect prediction, GWAS + ML, polygenic risk scores. AlphaFold 2/3, AlphaGenome and modern protein/DNA foundation models; learn structure prediction, biomolecular interactions and generative design.

3.1 Computational Neuroscience
Before you can build AI for the brain, you need a deep quantitative model of how the brain actually works. This is the science that feeds the engineering.
Single Neuron Models
Hodgkin-Huxley equations (the original neural spiking model), Integrate-and-Fire models, adaptive exponential IF. Simulating neurons in code using NEURON or Brian2. Understanding why approximations work.
BiophysicsSpikingNEURON
Network & Systems Neuroscience
Hopfield networks, attractor dynamics, working memory models, the free energy principle (Karl Friston's predictive coding). How circuits give rise to perception, cognition, and behaviour.
NetworksDynamicsCognition
Spike Sorting & Neural Data Analysis
Multi-electrode arrays, spike sorting (Kilosort, MountainSort), population vector decoding, dimensionality reduction of neural trajectories (PCA, UMAP on firing rates). The raw data pipeline from electrode to insight.
SpikesKilosortPopulation
Reinforcement Learning as Neuroscience
Temporal difference learning as a model of dopamine signals. Prediction error in the basal ganglia. The deep connection between RL and reward circuits — why understanding one illuminates the other.
RLDopamineBasal Ganglia
Resources
Theoretical Neuroscience — Dayan & Abbott Neuromatch Academy (free online) Allen Brain Atlas (open data) Brian2 / NEST simulators
3.2 Brain-Computer Interfaces & Neural Decoding
Reading thought and intent from brain signals. From EEG headbands to Neuralink. The most direct application of AI to the nervous system.
EEG-Based BCIs
Motor imagery classification (left vs right hand), P300 speller, SSVEP paradigms. EEGNet (compact CNN for EEG), ERP components (N200, P300). MOABB benchmark for EEG MI. The most accessible entry point into BCI.
EEGMotor ImageryEEGNet
Invasive BCI — The Frontier
Utah arrays, electrocorticography (ECoG), Neuropixels probes. BrainGate trials (speech decoding, cursor control). Meta's research decoding speech from MEG. Neuralink's first human implant results. Know the engineering AND the neuroscience here.
ECoGNeuropixelsBrainGate
Neural Speech & Language Decoding
Decoding attempted speech from paralysed patients (Frank Willett / Chang Lab papers). RNN and Transformer decoders trained on neural population activity. GPT-based language models integrated with neural readout. One of the most impactful BCI directions.
SpeechParalysisTransformers
Neurostimulation & Closed-Loop Systems
Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) for Parkinson's, TMS, tDCS. Closed-loop systems that decode neural state and deliver stimulation in response. Adaptive DBS — the next generation. Medtronic, Abbott, and academic research.
DBSClosed-LoopTMS
Transfer Learning Across BCI Sessions
The non-stationarity problem: neural signals change day-to-day, electrode-to-electrode. Domain adaptation, meta-learning (MAML), and Riemannian geometry-based alignment for EEG. A critical practical challenge in BCI deployment.
Domain AdaptationMeta-learningRiemannian
Key Papers & Tools
MNE-Python (EEG/MEG) MOABB (BCI benchmark) Willett et al. 2023 + current neural speech decoding literature BrainGate publications Braindecode library
3.3 AI for Neurological & Psychiatric Disease
Billion-dollar problems with inadequate tools. AI is beginning to change diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment selection for the most complex diseases humans face.
Alzheimer's & Dementia AI
Amyloid PET classification, structural MRI hippocampal atrophy, CSF biomarker modelling (Aβ42/tau ratio), speech and language markers of cognitive decline, multi-modal progression prediction. ADNI dataset is your anchor.
Alzheimer'sADNIMultimodal
Parkinson's Disease AI
Gait analysis (accelerometry + ML), voice/speech markers (jitter, shimmer, MFCCs), DaTscan SPECT imaging, tremor quantification from wearables. Remote monitoring for disease progression tracking.
Parkinson'sGaitVoice
Epilepsy Seizure Detection & Prediction
Automated seizure detection from scalp EEG (TUH EEG corpus — largest in the world), intracranial EEG seizure prediction, spike detection. Deep learning vs traditional feature engineering. FDA-cleared devices in this space.
EpilepsySeizureTUH EEG
Mental Health & Psychiatry AI
Depression/anxiety classification from speech, facial action units, actigraphy. Suicide risk stratification from clinical notes. Treatment response prediction for antidepressants. Digital biomarkers from smartphones. An emerging but ethically complex domain.
DepressionDigital BiomarkersEthics
Stroke & TBI
Ischaemic stroke lesion segmentation from DWI MRI, large vessel occlusion detection for triage (Viz.ai — FDA cleared), prognosis after TBI from CT, rehabilitation monitoring with wearable movement sensors.
StrokeTBITriage
3.4 Advanced Neuroimaging AI
fMRI and diffusion MRI contain structural and functional maps of the entire brain. AI is learning to read them in ways that took humans decades to begin understanding.
fMRI Connectivity & Resting-State
Default Mode Network, functional connectomes, graph-based models of brain connectivity, fMRI decoding (reading visual images from brain activity — mind-reading research). Nilearn and nitime for analysis.
fMRIConnectomeDecoding
Diffusion Tractography
DTI (Fractional Anisotropy, Mean Diffusivity), white matter tract reconstruction (FSL, MRtrix3), deep learning tractography. Mapping the structural wiring of the brain — damaged in MS, TBI, and schizophrenia.
DTIWhite MatterMRtrix3
Graph Neural Networks for Brain
Brain as a graph: nodes = regions, edges = functional or structural connectivity. GNNs for connectome-based disease classification. BrainNetCNN, BrainGB. The most principled way to model brain network data.
GNNConnectomeBrainNetCNN
Tools & Datasets
Nilearn (fMRI Python) FSL / FreeSurfer / MRtrix3 Human Connectome Project (HCP) ADNI / PPMI / TUH EEG OpenNeuro.org (BIDS datasets)

4.1 Research — Read, Reproduce, Extend
You become known in research by producing research. Start by deeply understanding the best existing work, then find the gap.
Paper Reading Practice
Read 2–3 papers per week in your focus areas. Use structured reading: Abstract → Figures → Methods → Results → Related Work. Write a 1-paragraph summary for each. Do this for 12 months and you will know the field.
HabitResearch
Reproduce a Key Paper
Pick one landmark paper (BraTS segmentation, EEGNet, neural speech decoding, AlphaFold application) and reproduce it completely from scratch. Reproducing is understanding. Publish the reproduction as a GitHub repo with a thorough README.
ReproductionGitHub
Find a Novel Contribution
Look for: (1) a method that works in domain A applied to domain B, (2) a dataset no one has properly benchmarked, (3) a known limitation explicitly called out in a paper's "future work" section. This is where your first paper lives.
Research GapContribution

The next wave of healthcare AI is large, general, multimodal foundation models. Know their architecture, limitations, and potential.
Medical Vision-Language Models
BioViL-T, CheXagent, LLaVA-Med, MedFlamingo — models that understand both images and clinical text. Trained on radiology reports + images. Contrastive learning (BioViL), instruction tuning. The radiology report generation pipeline.
VLMRadiologyCLIP

USMLE performance benchmarks, clinical QA, chain-of-thought medical reasoning. Fine-tuning on clinical guidelines. Evaluating hallucination in medical contexts — a critical safety problem. Retrieval augmented generation for clinical accuracy.
LLMSafetyRAG
Federated Learning for Healthcare
Training on distributed hospital data without data leaving institutions. PySyft, Flower, NVIDIA FLARE. The primary solution to privacy-preserving multi-site training — essential for any global health AI deployment.
FederatedPrivacyFLARE
Synthetic Medical Data Generation
Diffusion models for synthetic chest X-rays (RoentGen), synthetic EHR (CTGAN, synthea), privacy-preserving data augmentation. Solving the rare disease data problem. FDA guidance on synthetic data for regulatory submissions.

5.2 Advanced Neuro AI — The Cutting Edge
The papers being published today in this domain. Read them, understand them, and eventually contribute to them.
Brain Decoding at Scale
MindEye2 (reconstructing images seen by subjects from fMRI), semantic speech decoding from ECoG. The challenge of generalising across subjects. Large-scale contrastive learning between brain and semantic space. Meta and Chang Lab's latest results.
DecodingfMRIFrontier
Neural Manifolds & Geometry
Population activity lives in low-dimensional manifolds. GPFA, LFADS (Latent Factor Analysis via Dynamical Systems), CEBRA (state-of-the-art self-supervised neural representation learning). The geometry of thought.
ManifoldsLFADSCEBRA
Organoids & Biological Neural Networks
Brain-on-a-chip, cortical organoids playing Pong (DishBrain). AI models trained on organoid activity. The intersection of synthetic biology and AI. An emerging, speculative, but scientifically serious frontier.

AI Neuroscience — What AI Tells Us About Brains
Using DNNs as models of the visual cortex. Neural network units matching V1/V4/IT responses. Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA). The reverse project: using AI to understand biology, not just apply it.
RSAVisual CortexNeuro-AI
5.3 Explainability, Safety & Clinical Validation
The best model is useless if a clinician cannot trust it and a regulator cannot approve it. Explainability is not optional — it is the job.
XAI for Medical Images
Grad-CAM, GradCAM++, SHAP for image models, Integrated Gradients. Attention rollout for Vision Transformers. Concept-based explanations (TCAV). Your visualisations should make clinical sense — not just numerical sense.
Grad-CAMSHAPAttention
Model Calibration & Uncertainty
Temperature scaling, conformal prediction, Monte Carlo Dropout for uncertainty estimation. A model that says "70% confident" should be right 70% of the time. In medicine, poorly calibrated confidence is dangerous.
CalibrationUncertaintyConformal
Clinical Validation Methodology
Prospective vs retrospective studies, TRIPOD reporting guidelines (for clinical prediction models), AUC, sensitivity/specificity trade-offs in clinical context, net benefit curves (decision curve analysis). The language that clinicians and regulators speak.
TRIPODValidationStatistics
