💼 Experienced Resume Template

Best for: 2+ years experience, mid-level roles

📌 Sections to Include

  • Header: Name, Contact, LinkedIn, Portfolio
  • Professional Summary: 2-3 lines highlighting experience
  • Work Experience: 3-5 bullet points per role (STAR method)
  • Skills: Technical stack, tools, soft skills
  • Projects/Products: Major deliverables
  • Education: Briefly at the bottom

✅ Tips for Experienced Roles

  • Focus on impact and metrics
  • Use STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
  • Show progression in responsibilities
  • Include leadership/mentorship examples
  • Remove "college projects" unless highly relevant
  • Keep to 2 pages maximum
