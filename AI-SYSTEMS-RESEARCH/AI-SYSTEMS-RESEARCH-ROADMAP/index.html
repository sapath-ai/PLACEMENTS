What sits on top of the foundation

Critical Path

15 reproductions, 5 phases

Depth over completeness. Every node spans a different layer on purpose — the goal is range, not just a stack of similar papers. Three nodes are flagged as extension candidates: the ones most likely to produce a genuinely original finding rather than a clean rerun.

The Actual Jump

Beyond reproduction

Reproduction is necessary infrastructure. It is not the thing that gets you into the room. What does: one or two reproductions that turn into something with a genuinely original finding, sitting on top of enough breadth that you don't read as one-dimensional. Below is the protocol for making that jump, and three concrete candidates pulled from the critical path.

  1. Choose a reproduction pair.
    Two things you've already built that share a variable worth isolating — same task, different method; same method, different scale; same architecture, different domain.
  2. State a falsifiable question.
    One that your specific reproduction — and not just the original papers — can answer with the compute you actually have. If the answer is already known at the scale you're testing, pick a different question.
  3. Run it as a controlled comparison.
    Matched data, matched compute budget, matched evaluation. Change exactly one variable. Anything less is an anecdote, not a finding.
  4. Report the failure modes honestly.
    What broke, what didn't generalize, what you expected and didn't see — this is usually the most informative part of the writeup, and the part people skip.
  5. Put it in front of people who'll push back.
    An advisor, a relevant research community, a workshop submission. A finding that never gets external pressure stays a hobby project, not a signal.
Note on scope: none of this is a substitute for identifying an open problem cold. It's the version of "original contribution" that's reachable from a reproduction roadmap in a bounded timeframe. It will not by itself get you to research-scientist-caliber novelty — it will get you a real answer to a real question that didn't exist before you ran the comparison, which is the actual floor for that next tier.
